# METHODS
## Data Sources
To investigate the molecular basis of Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis 1 (ALS1), we retrieved the complete wild-type coding sequence (CDS) and corresponding amino acid sequence of human superoxide dismutase 1 (SOD1) from the NCBI Nucleotide and Gene databases (RefSeq NM_000454.5, Gene ID 6647). Disease annotations, locus mapping, and phenotypic data for ALS1 and the p.Ala4Val (A4V) mutation were obtained from the Online Mendelian Inheritance in Man (OMIM #105400) and UniProtKB databases (UniProt ID: P00441, Disease ID: DI-00108). Primary functional details regarding human Cu/Zn-SOD enzymatic activity were documented using key literature indexed in PubMed (PMID: 18948262, PMID: 24140062, PMID: 36630448). To evaluate sequence alignment and evolutionary conservation across mammalian species, orthologous CDS and protein records for Pongo abelii (Gene ID: 100170281, RefSeq NM_001131969.1) and Bos taurus (Gene ID: 281495) were collected from NCBI Gene. Finally, three-dimensional crystallographic coordinates for the human wild-type Cu/Zn-SOD homodimer were downloaded from the RCSB Protein Data Bank (PDB ID: 2C9V).

## Implementation
We executed a pairwise nucleotide sequence alignment between the wild-type SOD1 CDS and the query mutant sequence using the Basic Local Alignment Search Tool for nucleotides (BLASTn) under default parameters to confirm the c.14C>T point mutation driving the A4V substitution. The nucleotide sequence was translated in silico using the standard genetic code to assess its impact on the open reading frame. For evolutionary comparative analysis, multiple sequence alignments (MSA) of both CDS and amino acid sequences across Homo sapiens, Pongo abelii, and Bos taurus were computed using Clustal Omega. We characterized the functional domains of the mature 154-amino-acid polypeptide, mapping the catalytic copper (Cu²⁺) site, the structural zinc (Zn²⁺) site, the intramolecular disulfide bridge, and the anti-parallel β-barrel motif. Lastly, we modeled the target p.Ala4Val substitution on the 3D protein structure (PDB ID: 2C9V) to evaluate changes in side-chain volume, spatial packing, and structural strain at the homodimer contact interface.

## Statistical Analysis
Sequence alignments and similarity metrics generated via BLASTn were evaluated based on Query Cover percentage, Percent Identity, and Expectation values (E-value), establishing statistical significance at an E-value threshold of 0.0. Evolutionary amino acid conservation and the biological plausibility of the A4V substitution across mammalian orthologs were scored using the BLOSUM62 substitution matrix, evaluating log-odds ratios to distinguish conservative from non-conservative replacements.
